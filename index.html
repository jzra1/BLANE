Adaptive Nutrition Driven by Real-Time Biometrics & Local Food Availability

BLANE is a clinical, context-aware nutrition management system tailored for the local Filipino environment. It calculates precise Mifflin-St Jeor daily targets, tracks local Tarlac City market stocks, and details meal decisions using Explainable AI (Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite).

The 9 Connected Pillars of BLANE

A synchronized system linking your health conditions directly to local food logistics

🔄

Real-Time Body Feedback Loop

Monitors daily weight, sleep, hydration, and active calories to dynamically adjust targets using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.

📈

Health Drift Detection

Analyzes 7-day biometric trends to spot negative patterns, score overall wellness, and suggest preventive lifestyle choices.

⚖️

Dynamic Portion Optimizer

Automatically scales recipe portions (0.5x - 2.0x) to match exact caloric and macronutrient targets for your goal.

Map

GeoMarket Ingredient Scanner

Maps local Tarlac markets (Public Market & SM Supermarket) showing live item stocks and ingredient availability.

₱

Price-Aware Meal Optimizer

Checks recipe costs against your custom PHP budget limits and suggests cheap, high-nutrition ingredient substitutes.

🍂

Seasonal Availability Engine

Identifies in-season Filipino crops and alerts you to swap out-of-season items with local, native alternatives.

🤖

AI-Powered Meal Explanation

Leverages Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite to output human-readable justifications explaining why each meal fits your criteria.

🛑

Constraint-Based System

Enforces food allergies (peanut, egg, dairy, seafood) and clinical diets (vegetarian, halal, diabetic, low sodium).

🍳

Recipe Recommendation

Renders complete culinary guides with DOST-FNRI PhilFCT verified nutrient metrics and cooking guides.
